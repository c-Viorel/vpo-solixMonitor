How to get your credentials

Option A – Direct account (recommended, App ≥ v3.10)

  1. Open the Anker app and add your C1000X.
  2. Use that same email + password in the form below.
  3. The country code is the 2-letter ISO country your account is registered in (us, de, gb, fr…)

Option B – Shared account (older App versions)

  1. Create a second Anker account with a different email.
  2. In the Anker app → My System → Share → invite the second account.
  3. Enter the second account's credentials here.

🔒 Credentials are AES-256 encrypted before being stored.
